Your game, your rules.

Enter each round’s point change, not a running total. Scores are whole numbers, including negatives. Roundslate adds them; it does not apply a game’s scoring rules, decide when a game ends, or resolve tied results.

Simple records. Explicit decisions.

Draft rounds preserve incomplete scores without counting them. Post only after entering every score explicitly. Reopening removes that round from standings until you post it again. Deleting a round requires it to be a draft; Undo is available during this session.

Local storage is limited to this origin, browser and device. No account, uploads, analytics or cloud sync. Only one tab should edit; conflict checks are best effort, not an atomic cross-tab lock.
